# Subprocessors

Last updated: :updated

These companies process personal data for FormHaven. We announce new or replaced subprocessors by email at least 30 days in advance, as set out in our [data processing agreement](/legal/dpa).

| Company | What they do for us | Data | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hetzner Online GmbH | The server where the app and database run | All account data and submissions | Germany (Falkenstein) |
| Cloudflare, Inc. (Email Service) | Sends submission and account emails | Recipient's email address and the email content, including submitted fields | Global network. EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses |
| Cloudflare, Inc. | DNS, TLS and protection against attacks. Requests, including form submissions, pass through its network in transit | IP addresses, request contents in transit | Global network. EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses |
| Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. | Payments for your subscription | Account holder's billing details, never form submissions | EU, with transfers under Standard Contractual Clauses |

Used only when you turn it on for a form, by saving a DidMyFormSend key on it:

| Company | What it does for you | Data | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| DidMyFormSend (didmyformsend.com), run by :entity | Alerts you when a form's emails stop going out. After each notification email we tell it whether the mail was accepted or refused | The mail service's name and failure kind, the time, and each notification address cut to its first character and domain plus a SHA-256 hash of the full address. A hash of an email address is still personal data. Nothing a visitor wrote is sent | Germany (Falkenstein) |

Remove the key from the form to stop it.

Google Ireland Limited is not on this list: it only receives your login when you choose "Continue with Google", and is responsible for that itself.
