# Privacy policy

Last updated: :updated

This policy covers the personal data of FormHaven account holders, people who report a form to us, and visitors to :site. Messages that visitors send through a customer's form, and the email addresses a customer adds to a form to receive them, are processed on that customer's behalf: for those, the customer is the controller and our [data processing agreement](/legal/dpa) applies. If you filled in a form on someone else's website, ask that website's owner about your data.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email address, hashed password | To run your account and send you submissions, notices when an email could not be delivered, and a weekly summary of your forms (you can turn the summary off) | Contract |
| When you accepted the terms | To show the terms were offered before the contract was made | Legitimate interest |
| If you choose "Continue with Google": your Google account id, name and verified email address, received from Google | To log you in without a password | Contract |
| Billing details (handled by Stripe; we never see your card number) | To charge your subscription and keep invoices | Contract, tax law |
| IP address and browser details in server logs | Security and abuse prevention | Legitimate interest |
| The pages of :site you visit while logged out, the site that linked you, and your country, device and browser, counted with Umami on our own server, plus how fast each page loads, any error a page runs into (emails and links removed) and the moment you create an account (only how you signed up, never your name or email). No cookies; nothing while you are logged in, or if your browser sends Do Not Track | To see which pages bring people to FormHaven, how many sign up, and which pages are slow or broken | Legitimate interest |
| Reports about a form: your name, email address and what you wrote | To handle the report and show what we decided, as the Digital Services Act requires | Legal obligation |
| Messages you send us | Support | Contract |

We do not sell personal data, show ads or use tracking cookies. We only set the cookies needed to log you in and protect our own pages against forgery.

## Who else is responsible

- **Google**, when you continue with Google, decides itself how it handles your Google account. See Google's privacy policy.
- **Stripe** processes your payment for us, and also uses payment data for its own purposes, such as fraud prevention and meeting financial regulation. For that part Stripe is responsible itself. See Stripe's privacy policy.

## How long we keep it

- Account data: until you delete your account, then removed straight away, including your forms, submissions, API keys and open sessions. Nightly backup copies on the same server hold it for up to 7 more days.
- Invoices: 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires. Stripe keeps them for us.
- Server logs: 14 days for application logs, up to 5 weeks for system logs.
- Site analytics (Umami): 2 years, then deleted automatically.
- Reports about a form: 2 years after the report, so we can show how we handled it.

## Who processes it for us

See the [subprocessor list](/legal/subprocessors). Data is stored in the EU. Cloudflare, which carries requests and emails through its global network, is a US company. Transfers to it rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Cloudflare is certified, and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

If you save a DidMyFormSend key on a form, FormHaven tells didmyformsend.com, also run by :entity, after each notification email whether the mail was accepted or refused, the mail service's name and failure kind, the time, and each notification address cut to its first character and domain plus a SHA-256 hash of the full address. A hash of an email address is still personal data. Nothing a visitor wrote is sent. Remove the key to stop.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, ask us to restrict its use, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email :email and we reply within 30 days. You can also delete your account yourself in Settings. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## Changes

We email account holders at least 30 days before a material change to this policy.
