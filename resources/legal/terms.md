## How we deal with abuse

- **Spam filtering.** Every submission is checked automatically, without a person looking at it: a hidden field that only bots fill in and, if you set them, the sites a form accepts posts from. A submission that fails a check is not refused but kept in the form's spam list with the reason, and is not emailed or sent to your webhook. On a form that keeps no submissions, posts caught by the hidden field are not kept at all. Separately, a visitor who sends more than 10 submissions a minute to one form is asked to wait.
- **Reports.** Anyone can report a form they believe is illegal or breaks these terms on our [report page](/report). We confirm receipt, look at every report by hand, and tell the reporter what we decided.
- **What we do.** If a form or account breaks these terms or the law, or endangers the service or other customers, we may pause the form, stop its emails, or suspend or close the account. We choose the lightest measure that stops the problem, and act at once only where waiting would cause harm.
- **Our reasons.** When we do this we email you what we did and for how long, the facts it is based on, whether it followed a report, which rule in these terms or which law it relies on, and how you can contest it. Reply to that email to contest it: a person reviews your reply, and you can also go to a Dutch court.
